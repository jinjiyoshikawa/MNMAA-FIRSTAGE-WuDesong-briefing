2. Artist background

Karl NG Tak Tsung works with performance, video, interactive systems and installation. His practice looks at how technological change reshapes consciousness, perception and social structure, and how physical materials, digital systems and light can make that condition felt in a room.

CityU SCM MFA artist note (4 May 2026):

“Through performance, video, interactive art and installation, Karl creates works that investigate the relationship between technological advancement and human consciousness. His practice explores diverse materials and technologies through these themes.”

Related public works:

  • Constituent — installation, November 2025 (NOSCOPE720 / Metropolis Set @ 01 Murray, 22 Nov 2025). Public caption: working with physical materials, digital systems and light to build immersive environments.
  • Signal Check — live performance, 8 May 2026, 20:30–20:45, Multimedia Theatre, Run Run Shaw Creative Media Centre, CityU. Listed on the SCM MFACM 2026 Graduation Show programme.

Title: FIRSTAGE — ProjectPlayaround Macau, Media Arts Exhibition and Performance
Dates: 31 July – 29 August 2026, 12:00–20:00, free
Venue: Macao Contemporary Art Center · Navy Yard No.1 (former D. Carlos I Dock Machine Room), Rua de S. Tiago da Barra / 澳門媽閣上街
Curator: Keith Lam 林欣傑

Organiser: Macao New Media Arts Association
Support: Dimension Plus; venue support by District Development Center

4. Work introduction — Storage 《斷留所》

Artists: Karl NG 吳德淞, Casber U 余家杰, Summer Ha 夏天翔, Mathew Hon Yun 韓檬
Form: collaborative media installation inside Navy Yard No.1

Organiser text (MNMAA Facebook, 24 August 2026):

When the moving wall under the fingertips stretches without end, everyday blank space is packed with shattered digital messages. Contemporary people are endlessly written into that stream, and gradually lose the capacity to sit with emptiness. Intuition and flashes of insight that once broke through in deep attention dissolve with every notification.

The work builds a tall “spiritual bomb shelter.” In contemporary noise — even a storm of interference — it forces a pause for fragile attention. Only when a person is willing to seal themselves inside this vacuum of “stopped input” can the inspirations pressed down by daily noise, hidden in the depths of consciousness, be triggered and awakened again.

Art Map summary: the group constructed a shelter one looks up at, letting visitors refuse the daily input of the electronic world and greet the self with a simple question: how are you?

Opening note: after the opening, Casber U embraced collaborators Karl NG (Hong Kong), Summer Ha and Mathew Hon Yun. Casber said the project’s point is to cross fields and talk with people unlike oneself; collaboration pushed him out of a fixed media-art aesthetic.

5. Closing performance

FIRSTAGE was conceived as exhibition and performance. Official materials describe six works and four performances across the run. The public programme closed on 29 August 2026. MNMAA listed a closing gathering as Ppm Party_0829 on its Linktree.

Karl NG presented a closing performance at the exhibition (artist confirmation; exact published title/time not found in press at time of compiling). Please treat this as the live activation of the Storage research — body, signal and attention inside the heritage machine hall — rather than a separate touring piece.
